    CFIHOS Standards

    November 2025 – Major release: Version 2.0 with Extended RDL

    Version 2.0 signifies the most extensive update to the specification to date

    The updates cover:

    *  RDL delivery simplification
    – Core
    –   Reduce the complexity by combining tabs (entities)
    –   Remove columns where it makes sense
    –   Remove terminated items
    –   No loss of fidelity
    – Extended
    –   All entities included – no views
    –   Terminated items included
    –   Provenance of items
    –   Full assembly required
    –   Same fidelity as Core version

    *  Refactoring of the specification document to support use of requirements management tools.
    *  Inclusion of specific attributes, UOM, and documents required to create a reliable inventory of GHG emissions from capital projects, with specific attention to scope 3.
    *  Aligned with the Energistics UOM standard
    *  Added ability to collect and handover Spares Information and recommendations.
    *  Significant RDL JIP33 alignment
    *  ISO 14224 Enrichment of CFIHOS
    *  General Bug fixes.
    *  Improved visibility of the relationships between entity and entity attributes.
    *  Amended equipment and tag class hierarchies.
    *  Expanded tag class and equipment class properties.
    *  Updated tag equipment class relationships to improve their accuracy.

    For more information please read the V2.0 Release Notes

    All documents are available to download and use FREE of charge.
